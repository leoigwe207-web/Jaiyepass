# JaiyePass — Event-Organizer Sales Call Pitch

**Use case:** outbound call or WhatsApp voice note to a Lagos event organizer / promoter.
Target length: 90 seconds spoken.

---

## The 60–90 second version

> "Hi [Name], I'm [your name] with JaiyePass — we help Lagos organizers sell tickets and get people
> through the door without the WhatsApp stress.
>
> Three things we do differently. **One:** your listing isn't just posted — it lands inside curated
> collections people actually browse, like 'Tonight in Lagos' and 'Under ₦10,000', so you get
> discovered by people who aren't following you yet.
>
> **Two:** friends plan together on the platform. Someone taps 'make a plan' on your event, shares
> one link, and the whole crew sees who's coming — that's group sales you'd otherwise chase in
> three group chats.
>
> **Three:** entry is fast and airtight. Every ticket is a signed QR code — screenshots can't be
> forwarded around, and your door team scans straight from their phone with an instant valid or
> rejected verdict. No lists on paper, no arguments at the gate.
>
> On the money side: you see live sales and exactly what you can withdraw, refunds are handled
> cleanly in one place, and our fee is 2.5% capped at ₦2,000 per ticket, charged to the buyer —
> ticket price stays yours.
>
> Onboarding takes two minutes, and I can get your first event reviewed and live this week. Can I
> send you a link to create your organizer account?"

---

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already sell on WhatsApp" | "Keep WhatsApp — we give you one shareable link that tracks every sale and puts your crew's plan in one place instead of screenshots." |
| "What does it cost me?" | "2.5% service fee, capped at ₦2,000, paid by the buyer. No monthly fee, no setup fee." |
| "When do I get my money?" | "You see available balance live, request a payout any time, and we review within one business day." |
| "My audience is on Instagram" | "That's the point — we send them a checkout that finishes in under a minute, and your event shows up in collections people browse for plans." |
| "I'm worried about fake tickets" | "Each ticket is a signed QR tied to one order; the scanner checks it cryptographically, so a forwarded screenshot dies at the door." |
| "What if the event is cancelled?" | "Refund handling is built in — one decision on your dashboard, attendee gets their money back and an email. Your reputation stays intact." |

## Discovery questions (use first if you have 2 minutes)

1. How many tickets did your last event sell, and how did you track them?
2. What happens at your door right now — list on phone, paper, or gate crashers?
3. Do groups usually buy together, or one person buys for everyone?
4. What did your last event cost you in platform fees, airtime and manual work?
